# Outgrown: Forever

Know when your gear has fallen behind, and spot upgrades as you find them.

Outgrown: Forever is a World of Warcraft: Forever addon that helps you keep track of your gear as you are leveling your way up to 60. It uses a deterministic algorithm and compares gear to what's most likely to fit your level, class, talent tree and attribute priorities.

---

## How it works

Outgrown works out your spec from your talents, then scores each item by the stats that spec cares about. A Retribution Paladin values Strength and some Intellect; a Holy Priest values Intellect and Spirit et cetera.

An item in your bags or a quest reward glows green when it beats what you're wearing in that slot, your class can use it, and your level allows it. Armor is only suggested in the best type your class wears, such as mail for a level 20 Paladin, unless what you're wearing in that slot is lighter still. Quest rewards may require up to 2 levels above you, since you'll often level before handing the quest in.

Nothing is graded below level 10, and items without a required level are left alone.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The scoring is an estimate, not a simulation. Trust your own judgement for special items.

---

### Caveats

- Items with effects the addon can't read, like trinkets with a "Use:" effect, are graded gently and marked _(some effects not rated)_.
- "Equip:" effects are only read on English game clients.
- Replacement bag addons such as Bagnon or AdiBags don't show the green glow.
- Upgrades are only found among items you can see: your bags, and quests you have or are being offered.
